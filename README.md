# ✈️ NextLeg

> Turning travel chaos into smarter journeys.

NextLeg is a flight disruption intelligence platform designed to help travelers quickly discover the best alternative routes during severe weather events and operational disruptions.

Inspired by a real experience during the 2024 hurricane season, NextLeg combines data engineering and machine learning to predict flight cancellations and recommend better travel options when passengers need them most.

## The Story Behind NextLeg

In August 2024, I was traveling to Las Vegas for a major conference when my flight was canceled because of a hurricane.

I found myself stranded in Philadelphia.

The customer service line stretched across the airport, and it took nearly three hours just to speak with an airline representative. Most travelers were being rebooked two or three days later.

Determined to make it to Las Vegas on time, I asked whether there were any connecting flights available—even if it meant taking a longer route.

Eventually, I was offered a 24-hour itinerary involving three separate stops.

I arrived just in time for the conference, but the experience exposed a major problem:

**Travelers have very limited visibility into alternative options during disruptions.**

That experience inspired NextLeg.

---

## What is NextLeg?

NextLeg helps travelers answer questions like:

- Which flights are most likely to be canceled?
- What alternative routes can get me to my destination faster?
- Should I wait for my original flight or rebook immediately?
- How can airlines proactively support affected passengers?
