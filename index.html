The Problem That Started It All

In August 2024, I was traveling to Las Vegas for a major conference when my flight was canceled because of a hurricane.

I found myself stranded in Philadelphia, waiting nearly three hours in a customer service line while hundreds of travelers searched for answers. Most people were told they would not be able to leave for another two or three days.

After asking about every possible alternative route, I eventually pieced together a 24-hour journey involving three separate flights that got me to Las Vegas just in time.

Walking away from that experience, one question stuck with me:

What if travelers knew which flights were at risk before cancellations happened?

Project Overview

🌪 Predict Disruptions

Estimate cancellation risk before severe weather impacts operations.

🛫 Smarter Rebooking

Enable proactive recommendations for alternative routes.

⏱ Better Experiences

Reduce airport uncertainty and long customer service waits.

What I Built

1. Data Collection

Used Python to collect and integrate data from multiple sources:

  • 🌦 Meteo weather data
  • ✈️ Bureau flight records
  • 🏢 Internal airport information

2. ETL Pipeline

Designed and implemented an end-to-end ETL pipeline to extract, transform, and load more than 2.5 million flight records into PostgreSQL.

3. Feature Engineering

Created predictive variables related to weather conditions, flight schedules, routes, airports, and operational patterns.

4. Machine Learning

Trained and evaluated multiple machine learning models using pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn to identify flights with elevated cancellation risk.

5. Practical Application

Connected predictions to the NextLeg concept by designing the system to support intelligent alternative flight recommendations.
