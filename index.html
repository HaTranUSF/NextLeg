The Story Behind NextLeg

In August 2024, I was traveling to Las Vegas for a major conference when my flight was canceled because of a hurricane.

I found myself stranded in Philadelphia, waiting nearly three hours in a customer service line while hundreds of travelers searched for answers. Most people were being told they would not be able to leave for another two or three days.

After asking about every possible alternative route, I eventually pieced together a 24-hour journey involving three separate flights that got me to Las Vegas just in time.

That experience inspired me to build NextLeg: a system designed to predict flight disruptions and help travelers quickly identify better alternatives before chaos unfolds.

What I Built

Data Integration

Used Python to collect and combine data from multiple sources, including Meteo weather data, Bureau flight records, and internal airport information.

ETL Pipeline

Designed and implemented an end-to-end ETL pipeline that extracted, transformed, and loaded more than 2.5 million flight records into PostgreSQL.

Feature Engineering

Engineered features related to weather conditions, flight routes, airport operations, and scheduling patterns.

Machine Learning

Trained and evaluated predictive models using pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn to identify flights with elevated cancellation risk.

Real-World Application

Designed the system so predictions could power NextLeg's vision of recommending alternative routes during travel disruptions.
